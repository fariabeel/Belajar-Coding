The Country

Nestled in a fantastical realm teeming with magic and mythical creatures, Axel is the bustling metropolis of the Kingdom of Axis.

This kingdom is a land of stark contrasts, where towering castles and quaint villages coexist amidst sprawling forests and treacherous dungeons. The citizens of Axis are a hardy and adventurous bunch, ever eager to seek their fortunes in the nearby dungeon or engage in spirited tavern brawls.
